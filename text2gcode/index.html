Turn text into CNC engraving toolpaths

1 Text

2 Font

Outline fonts support boss & pocket modes. Single-stroke fonts are made for engraving.

3 Engraving mode

Leaves the letters as a raised boss: the tool traces around all letters considered together, offset outward by the tool radius. Overlapping letters merge — no cut between them.

4 Tool & fill

Boss & pocket automatically offset by the tool radius (half the tool diameter), so the tool edge lands exactly on the letter boundary.

Rings follow the letter outlines, stepping inward by the stepover until the pocket is cleared — the tool never leaves the letter shape.
